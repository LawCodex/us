## Subpart M—Organization

### § 5.1100 Agency Organization Information.

Information about the organization of the Food and Drug Administration (including its central and field offices) is available on the Agency's website at *http://www.fda.gov,* including in FDA's Staff Manual Guides. Relevant contact information for Agency offices, including email addresses, is also available on the Agency's website.
