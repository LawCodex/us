# PART 1235—SAFETY STANDARD FOR BABY CHANGING PRODUCTS

**Authority:** 15 U.S.C. 2056a; Sec 3, Pub. L. 112-28, 125 Stat. 273.

**Source:** 83 FR 29861, June 26, 2018, unless otherwise noted.

## § 1235.1 Scope.

This part establishes a consumer product safety standard for baby changing products.

## § 1235.2 Requirements for baby changing products.

Each baby changing product shall comply with all applicable provisions of ASTM F2388-21, *Standard Consumer Safety Specification for Baby Changing Products for Domestic Use,* approved on November 15, 2021. The Director of the Federal Register approves this incorporation by reference in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 552(a) and 1 CFR part 51.
