# PART 1233—SAFETY STANDARD FOR PORTABLE HOOK-ON CHAIRS

**Authority:** 15 U.S.C. 2056a.

**Source:** 81 FR 17065, Mar. 28, 2016, unless otherwise noted.

## § 1233.1 Scope.

This part establishes a consumer product safety standard for portable hook-on chairs.

## § 1233.2 Requirements for portable hook-on chairs.

Each portable hook-on chair must comply with all applicable provisions of ASTM F1235-25, *Standard Consumer Safety Specification for Portable Hook-On Chairs,* approved November 15, 2025. The Director of the Federal Register approves the incorporation by reference listed in this section in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 552(a) and 1 CFR part 51.
