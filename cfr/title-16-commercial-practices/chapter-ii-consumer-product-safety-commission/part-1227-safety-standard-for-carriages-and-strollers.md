# PART 1227—SAFETY STANDARD FOR CARRIAGES AND STROLLERS

**Authority:** The Consumer Product Safety Improvement Act of 2008, Pub. L. 110-314, § 104, 122 Stat. 3016 (August 14, 2008); Pub. L. 112-28, 125 Stat. 273 (August 12, 2011).

**Source:** 79 FR 13216, Mar. 10, 2014, unless otherwise noted.

## § 1227.1 Scope.

This part establishes a consumer product safety standard for carriages and strollers.

## § 1227.2 Requirements for carriages and strollers.

Each carriage and stroller shall comply with all applicable provisions of ASTM F833-21, Standard Consumer Safety Performance Specification for Carriages and Strollers, approved June 15, 2021. The Director of the Federal Register approves this incorporation by reference in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 552(a) and 1 CFR part 51.
