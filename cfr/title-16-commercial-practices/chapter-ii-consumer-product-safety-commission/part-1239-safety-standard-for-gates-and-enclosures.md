# PART 1239—SAFETY STANDARD FOR GATES AND ENCLOSURES

**Authority:** 15 U.S.C. 2056a.

**Source:** 85 FR 40112, July 6, 2020, unless otherwise noted.

## § 1239.1 Scope.

This part establishes a consumer product safety standard for gates and enclosures.

## § 1239.2 Requirements for gates and enclosures.

Each gate and enclosure must comply with all applicable provisions of ASTM F1004-25, *Standard Consumer Safety Specification for Expansion Gates and Expandable Enclosures,* approved on December 1, 2025.
