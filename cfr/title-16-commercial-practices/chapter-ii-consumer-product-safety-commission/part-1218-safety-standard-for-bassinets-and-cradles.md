# PART 1218—SAFETY STANDARD FOR BASSINETS AND CRADLES

**Authority:** 15 U.S.C. 2056a.

**Source:** 78 FR 63034, Oct. 23, 2013, unless otherwise noted.

## § 1218.1 Scope.

This part establishes a consumer product safety standard for bassinets and cradles.

[90 FR 57695, Dec. 12, 2025]

## § 1218.2 Requirements for bassinets and cradles.

Each bassinet and cradle must comply with all applicable provisions of ASTM F2194-25. ASTM F2194-25, Standard Consumer Safety Specification for Bassinets and Cradles, approved August 1, 2025, which is incorporated by reference into this section with the approval of the Director of the Federal Register under 5 U.S.C. 552(a) and 1 CFR part 51.
