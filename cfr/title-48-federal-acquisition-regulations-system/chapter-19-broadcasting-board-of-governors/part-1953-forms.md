# PART 1953—FORMS

**Editorial Note:**

IAAR forms referenced in this subpart do not appear in the Code of Federal Regulations. The list of forms following 1953.370 is set forth for the convenience of the user. Forms may be obtained by writing: Office of Contracts, The Broadcasting Board of Governors, Washington, DC 20547.

**Authority:** 40 U.S.C. 486(c).

**Source:** 50 FR 13224, Apr. 3, 1985, unless otherwise noted.

This subpart contains illustrations of some forms referenced in this IAAR.
