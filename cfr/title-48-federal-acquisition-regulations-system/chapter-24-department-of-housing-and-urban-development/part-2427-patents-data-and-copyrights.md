# PART 2427—PATENTS, DATA, AND COPYRIGHTS

**Authority:** 40 U.S.C. 121(c); 42 U.S.C. 3535(d).

**Source:** 53 FR 46536, Nov. 17, 1988, unless otherwise noted.

## Subpart 2427.3—Patent Rights Under Government Contracts

### 2427.305 Administration of patent rights clauses.

### 2427.305-2 Administration by the Government.

(b) Contractor reports. Contractors shall complete and submit to the Contracting Officer HUD Form 770, Report of Inventions and Subcontracts, upon receipt of said form. The Contracting Officer shall send the form to those contractors whose contract work may have required the development of inventions upon physical completion of the contract.

## Subpart 2427.4—Rights in Data and Copyrights

### 2427.470 Contract clause.

The contracting officer shall insert the clause 2452.227-70, Government Information, in all solicitations and contracts when the Government will provide information to the contractor, and/or when the contractor will obtain information on the Government's behalf to perform work required under the contract. The contracting officer shall describe all information to be provided to the contractor in paragraph (d)(1) of the clause.
