*Title 48—Federal Acquisition Regulations System › CHAPTER 6—DEPARTMENT OF STATE › SUBCHAPTER F—SPECIAL CATEGORIES OF CONTRACTING*

# PART 639—ACQUISITION OF INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY

**Authority:** 40 U.S.C. 486(c); 22 U.S.C. 2658.

**Source:** 72 FR 51569, Sept. 10, 2007, unless otherwise noted.

## Subpart 639.1—General

### 639.107 Contract clause.

### 639.107-70 DOSAR solicitation provision and contract clause.

(a) The contracting officer shall insert the provision at 652.239-70, Information Technology Security Plan and Accreditation, in solicitations that include information technology resources or services in which the contractor will have physical or electronic access to Department information that directly supports the mission of the Department.

(b) The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 652.239-71, Security Requirements for Unclassified Information Technology Resources, in solicitations and contracts containing the provision at 652.239-70. The provision and clause shall not be inserted in solicitations and contracts for personal services with individuals.
